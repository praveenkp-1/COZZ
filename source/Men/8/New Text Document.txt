CERTIFIED MATERIALS
RCS CERTIFIED RECYCLED POLYESTER
Nowadays, recycled polyester is mainly made from PET plastic waste. This is a type of plastic
that is widely used in a variety of items, such as plastic bottles. Using recycled materials 
helps limit the production of virgin polyester fiber. It is certified to the Recycled Claim
Standard (RCS), which verifies the recycled content and tracks it from source to final product.
Certified by Intertek 193341

RCS CERTIFIED RECYCLED COTTON
This fiber is made from recycled cotton textile waste. Using recycled materials helps limit the
consumption of raw materials. It is certified to the Recycled Claim Standard (RCS), which verifies
the recycled content and tracks it from source to final product.

CARE
Caring for your clothes is caring for the environment.
Lower temperature washes and delicate spin cycles are gentler on garment, helping to maintain the color, shape and structure of the fabric. At the same time it reduces energy consumption that is used in care processes.
