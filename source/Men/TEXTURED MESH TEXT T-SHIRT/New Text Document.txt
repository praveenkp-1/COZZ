RCS CERTIFIED RECYCLED POLYESTER
Nowadays, recycled polyester is mainly made from PET plastic waste. This is a type of plastic that is widely used in
a variety of items, such as plastic bottles. Using recycled materials helps limit the production of virgin polyester
fiber. It is certified to the Recycled Claim Standard (RCS), which verifies the recycled content and tracks it from
source to final product.

Caring for your clothes is caring for the environment.

shape and structure of the fabric. At the same time it reduces energy consumption that is used in care processes.
